What we collect, what we do not, and how advertising works

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Short version: we do not ask for your name, email, or an account. Advertising is served by third parties who may set their own cookies. We store nothing on your device.

Who we are

This website is an adult video aggregation and indexing service. This policy explains what happens to data when you visit. It covers this website only; the third-party players and advertising networks we work with have their own policies.

Data we do not collect

To be explicit, because most adult sites collect far more than they need:

  • We do not require an account, and there is no login.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth.
  • We do not request or store government identity documents.
  • We do not sell personal data to third parties.
  • We do not operate a comment system or any feature that stores user-submitted content.

Cookies and local storage

We set no first-party cookies and write nothing to your browser's local storage. There is no preference, session, or tracking value stored by this website.

Third-party advertising networks and the embedded video players may set their own cookies or use similar technologies. Those are described in the sections below.

Advertising and cookies

This site is funded by advertising. We work with third-party advertising networks that serve popunder and display advertisements. Those networks may set cookies or use similar technologies to:

  • Limit how often you see the same advertisement;
  • Measure impressions and detect fraudulent traffic; and
  • Select advertisements based on the content of the page you are viewing.

We do not control the cookies those networks set, and their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Advertising partners

Current partners include JuicyAds (juicyads.com). Where additional networks are added, this page will be updated to list them.

Embedded video players

Videos are displayed through embedded players hosted by third-party platforms. When you load a video page, and again when you press play, your browser connects directly to that third-party platform. That platform will see your IP address, your browser and device details, and may set its own cookies.

We do not receive or store that data. The privacy policy of the originating platform governs what it collects — for videos embedded from xnxx.com, that is the XNXX privacy policy.

Server logs

Our hosting and content-delivery providers automatically record standard technical data for every request: IP address, timestamp, requested URL, HTTP status, referrer, and user-agent string. This is generated by the infrastructure, not by tracking code we wrote. It is used for security, abuse prevention, and diagnosing faults, and is retained for a limited period consistent with the provider's log retention.

We do not attempt to link log entries to individual identities, and we do not build user profiles from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK), CCPA/CPRA (California), or similar regimes. Because we hold no account data and no identifiers tied to you, most such requests are satisfied by clearing your browser storage. That said:

  • Access: we hold no personal record to disclose.
  • Erasure: we store nothing on your device, so there is nothing to erase on our side. Clear your browser storage to remove any cookies set by our advertising partners.
  • Objection to advertising cookies: use your browser's tracking-protection settings, a content blocker, or the opt-out tools each advertising network provides.
  • Complaints: you may contact us at the address above, or your local data-protection authority.

Children

This website is restricted to adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect any data from minors. If you believe a minor has accessed this site, please use our Report Content page.
