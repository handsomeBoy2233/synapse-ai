What to include

To let us act quickly, please include:

  • The full URL of the video page on this site, copied from your browser's address bar;
  • What the problem is, in one or two sentences;
  • If you are reporting non-consensual imagery, confirmation that you are the person depicted, or are authorised to act for them; and
  • Any supporting detail that helps us verify — but do not send identity documents.

You may report anonymously. We will still review the entry, but we may be unable to confirm the outcome back to you.

Where the video actually lives

This site indexes and embeds videos; it does not host them. Delisting a page here removes it from our catalogue, but the underlying video continues to exist on the platform that published it. For full removal, also report the video to that platform — it is identified in the player frame on the video page (most videos here come from xnxx.com).

We strongly recommend doing both. Our delisting is immediate but local; the platform's removal is what actually takes the material down.

Law enforcement and child-safety organisations

Reports involving minors are preserved and, where US law applies, referred to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC). If you are reporting an emergency or an imminent risk to a person, contact your local police first — we cannot respond to emergencies.

You can also report directly to NCMEC through their reporting channel, or to the Internet Watch Foundation if you are in the UK.
